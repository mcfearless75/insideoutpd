Local, Not Just Nearby

Birkenhead is where we started

Being based on Price St means a Birkenhead job doesn't involve a long drive, a subcontractor, or a call centre — it's us, popping round to have a look, usually within a day or two of you getting in touch.

We've worked right across the town: the Victorian terraces around Birkenhead Park and Hamilton Square, the 1930s semis further towards Prenton and Oxton, and newer builds near Woodside and the docks. Each of those brings different challenges — older solid-wall terraces need different plastering and damp handling than a 1930s cavity-wall semi — and we've priced and built for all of them.
